Frequently Asked Questions

Should I hang drywall horizontally or vertically?

For residential walls with 8-foot or 9-foot ceilings, hanging sheets horizontally is recommended by professional tapers. It reduces total linear seam length by about 25% and places the main joint at comfortable waist height for taping and sanding.

What drywall screws should I use?

For 1/2-inch drywall into standard wood studs, use 1-1/4 inch coarse thread drywall screws spaced every 12 inches on ceilings and 16 inches on walls.
